ContribQuest

Your GitHub graph is haunted.

  • Every day is a tile. Your last 13 weeks, laid out exactly like your graph.
  • Busy days fight back. Monsters rise from them, labeled with the real date and count.
  • Your best day is the boss. It waits after the last week. Your streak is your armor.
  • Every move is a timing test. Strike in the gold zone for a critical. Only a perfect press dodges a hit completely.

Each week, step up, ahead, or down with W, D, S or the arrow keys. In a fight press A to attack, then Space at the right moment.
Your username is sent to a public community API that reads your contribution calendar. Nothing else leaves your browser.
